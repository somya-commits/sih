🆘 Emergency Instructions

  1. Move to the highest floor or nearest shelter immediately.
  2. Never walk, drive or wade through floodwater.
  3. Disconnect electrical appliances and turn off gas cylinders.
  4. Carry torch, first-aid kit, drinking water and important documents.
  5. Follow SDRF, NDRF and local administration instructions only.
  6. Call 112 (National Emergency) or 1078 (Disaster Helpline) if trapped.

Broadcast to villagers via siren, SMS and voice calls in the affected sector.
